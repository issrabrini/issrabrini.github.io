# Surface brief: portfolio home (index.html)

Mode: Experience. The work (six projects) leads from the first viewport.
Audience and action: recruiters for a Paris AI/ML internship; open a project, then CV or email.
Proof on hand: real metrics and links, no screenshots. Constraints: single static index.html, three.js from CDN, all copy and project dialog behavior preserved, usable without WebGL.
Memorable moment: glossy 3D tokens lift out of their tiles and tilt toward the pointer; keys 1 to 6 open projects.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a Paris Métro tile wall and each project is a coloured line. It refuses the screenshot card grid under a hero with a floating blob.

OWN-WORLD: Bevelled white running-bond ceramic tile ground with faint grout. Deep enamel navy signs with white caps. Six re-tuned line colours (yellow, blue, green, magenta, orange, violet) as roundels and fills, never copied from RATP. Archivo, bold and slightly wide, as signage type. One glossy resin 3D token per project rendered with three.js.

STORY: A recruiter sees at once an AI engineer who ships agents, with all six projects in view. They press or click one line, read the result and the case study, then take the CV or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440x900): Navy station-name sign header with the CV button. Below it a stacked intro band: headline "I build AI agents that do real work." and status on the left, lede with CV and Email buttons on the right.
