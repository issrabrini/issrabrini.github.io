Juriq: scheduling thesis-defense juries with AI agents

February to June 2026

Every year, someone builds the defense schedule by hand: 53 projects, 21 professors and 80 time slots, with unavailabilities, conflicts of interest and fairness to respect. It takes days, and it’s never quite fair.

Juriq lets administrators describe changes in plain language. An LLM agent turns them into constraints, a constraint solver guarantees the hard rules are never broken, and a reviewing agent explains what could still be better. When no schedule is possible, the system diagnoses why and proposes which rule to relax.

Rules can be switched on, off or reweighted at runtime without touching the code, and schedules export to PDF, Excel, Word and CSV.

Python, LangGraph, OR-Tools CP-SAT, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, React, Docker

Detecting lung disease from breathing sounds

April to May 2026

I reproduced a published deep-learning method that classifies breathing cycles as normal, crackle or wheeze, then set out to beat it.

  • Adapted an Audio Spectrogram Transformer and added targeted data augmentation, raising sensitivity from 0.68 to 0.75.
  • Found a structural limit in the single-output design and rebuilt it in two stages: first detect anything abnormal, then name the type.

PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, SpecAugment, SAM optimizer, Focal Loss
